Polonium is a chemical element with the symbol Po and atomic number 84. Marie and Pierre Curie discovered it in July 1898 while separating the components of uranium ore, and it was the first element found by following its radioactivity. Marie Curie named it after her native country, Poland, which at the time was divided among neighbouring empires.

Polonium is extremely radioactive and highly toxic. Its most common isotope, polonium-210, has a half-life of about 138 days, so it occurs in nature only in tiny traces.
